The approach

Fast, because nobody cares in three weeks.

Events don't repeat themselves, so there's no reshoot if we miss it. The job is staying close to the actual energy of the room rather than a stiff, staged version of it — and turning the edit around while people still remember the night happened. Society ball, store launch, festival stall, doesn't matter — same brief every time. Nobody's paying to watch someone find their camera settings mid-event — that bit happens at home, beforehand.
