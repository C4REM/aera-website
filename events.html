    Tell us what it is, how long it runs and where the content is going. We’ll say honestly if half a day is enough.

  2. 18:00 · Doors

    In before the guests

    The room while it’s still clean: the setup, the details, the things the venue spent money on, before three hundred people fill it.

  3. 20:30 · The middle of it

    Moving with it

    Working around the event rather than stopping it every five minutes for one more shot. Nobody is paying to watch someone find their settings.

  4. +4 days · After

    Stills and a short recap film, turned around while it’s still worth posting rather than three weeks later when nobody cares.
