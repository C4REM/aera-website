Week one

A call and a plan

We talk about what’s coming up for you that month, then agree a simple content calendar so shoot day isn’t spent deciding things.

One shoot day

Stills and reels in one focused session. We’re not turning up every week to get in your staff’s way.

Week three

The edit

Everything cut to the same look, captions drafted, and sent over for a once-over.

Week four

Posting

Ready-to-post files, or scheduled for you if you’d rather never open the app.
