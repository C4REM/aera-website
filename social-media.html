The approach

Fewer, stronger posts.

Most small business feeds don't fail because they post too little — they fail because they post whatever, whenever, with zero thought for how it all sits together. We do the opposite: fewer, better posts, shot and edited to one consistent style, so the account looks like it belongs to a brand twice its actual size. If a post isn't good enough to stop a scroll, it doesn't go up — even if that leaves a gap in the calendar that week.
