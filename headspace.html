Headspace

A psychological thriller about Nolan and the other versions of him. Written, directed, shot and edited by Jack Careem.

The film

Headspace is about Nolan and the other versions of him. Most scenes have several of him on screen at once: arguing with himself across a stage, riding three lifts side by side, standing in every doorway of a red corridor.

It plays like a thriller about multiple personalities, but it's really about mental health, and about telling someone when something's wrong instead of carrying it on your own. Fight Club was the reference: you're stuck inside one person's version of events, and you can't fully trust it.

Making it

It started as a school project in 2025 and ended up taking me over a year. I wrote it, directed it, shot it and edited it, with a friend helping on set.

Doing every job meant every problem was mine. If two shots didn't cut together, there was no camera operator to blame. Most of the tension in the final cut came from holding shots longer and showing less, and I only worked that out in the edit.

The scenes with several Nolans in one frame were built in After Effects, layer by layer. They took the longest of anything in the film, and they're why it won Best Editing at Dune.

Awards and screenings

In February 2026 Headspace won the first UAE Student Film Award at the 14th Black Sheep Film Festival, SAE Dubai's festival at the Paramount Hotel. It was the first year the festival opened up to students from across the country, not just SAE's own. It also won Best Editing at the Dune Creative Festival.

Next it screens at HollyShorts MENA in Dubai, 14 to 17 January 2027, the regional edition of the Oscar and BAFTA qualifying HollyShorts festival.
