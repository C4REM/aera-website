The award

The Black Sheep Film Festival is SAE University College Dubai's own festival, and for most of its life it was a showcase for SAE students. Its fourteenth edition, at the Paramount Hotel in Business Bay in February 2026, opened the doors for the first time: a new UAE Student Film Award, with entries invited from students across the country and ten films from outside SAE picked for official screening.

Headspace won it, so it's the award's first winner. It also took Best Editing at the Dune Creative Festival.
