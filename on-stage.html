On Stage

Shot from the stalls and the wings, during the real show.

Discipline
Theatre photography
Shot by
Toosh Alzaeem
Notes
Live performances, not photocalls

The job

Toosh shoots theatre during actual performances rather than a staged photocall, so what you see is the show itself: the stage lights, the costumes, and a cast who are clearly having the time of their lives.

How

She works from the stalls and the wings, so the cast and crew come away with pictures they can relive the night with. Nothing posed, nothing re-staged afterwards.

Photographed by Toosh Alzaeem. More of her work at madebyzoosh.
