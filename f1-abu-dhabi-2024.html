F1 Abu Dhabi 2024

Three days at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix. I'll be honest — I came for the concerts.

Discipline
Event photography
Year
2024
Role
Photographer
Notes
Yas Marina

The weekend

“I am an artist. The track is my canvas and my car is my brush.” — Graham Hill. Very noble. I came for the concerts.

What I got was a crash course in shooting things that move faster than the autofocus would like, in light that changes completely between the afternoon sessions and the night race. Cars, crowds, pit lane, and a stage after dark — four different shooting problems in one weekend.
