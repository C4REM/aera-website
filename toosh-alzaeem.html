Shoots theatre, gigs, weddings and anything with a crowd and some energy in the room. Four years in, starting out in Dubai and now based in Bournemouth, and happy to travel for a good job. On independent film sets she has run the lighting as head of gaffing and shot the behind-the-scenes photography. Never poses anyone stiffly; she catches what is actually happening and keeps the mood light, because people having fun always look their best.
