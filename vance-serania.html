Photographer and videographer, and the reason Aera can work in the Gulf rather than just talk about it. Came to it from a family of sculptors, sketch artists and designers, started shooting on a phone, then bought a Sony and never really stopped. Picked up cinematography, lighting and grading through a media course and has been taking freelance jobs around school ever since: brand films for Rolls-Royce and KIA Motors, boxing, fashion, and a fair amount of nobody-asked-me-to short film work. Cuts in Premiere and DaVinci.
