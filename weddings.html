The approach

We try very hard not to be annoying about it.

Weddings never go entirely to plan, and that's fine — the job isn't to control the day, it's to be close enough to catch it. No hour-long disappearing act for "creative shots" while your guests wonder where the photographer went. Just someone who knows when to get out of the way, and when to actually be useful. Nobody wants a stressed-out photographer adding to the chaos — least of all this one.
