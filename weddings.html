The approach

Quiet, considered, unobtrusive.

Weddings move fast and rarely go exactly to plan — the job is to stay close, stay calm, and come away with a set of images and a film that feel like the day actually did. No stage-managed poses, no disappearing for an hour of "creative shots." Just a team that knows when to step back and when to be ready.
