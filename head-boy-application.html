Head Boy Application

A film made to get a job I actually wanted. Every other candidate wrote a letter.

Discipline
Personal film
Year
2025
Role
Everything
Notes
Self-shot

The pitch

Going for Head Boy, I made a video instead of writing the standard application: who I am, what I care about, and what I'd actually do with the role if the school handed it over. Informative, but not a CV read aloud.

Why a film

If I was going to have any chance, I had to show who I was, what I could do, and what separated me from everyone else applying. A letter can't do that, and it can't show that I'll try something odd rather than the safe version.

Making the case in the medium I actually work in was the point. It was also a reasonable bet that nobody else would.
