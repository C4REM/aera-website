The studio Leeds & Dubai

Started in a student house in Leeds. Now, Leeds to Dubai.

Aera didn’t start with a business plan. It started with a camera, a lot of frames that missed, and a stubborn belief that good media shouldn’t be reserved for the brands that can afford an agency.

The overhead is a laptop, a hard drive, and a mild caffeine dependency. What that buys you isn’t only a smaller invoice. It’s someone who has time to sit with your edit until it’s actually right.
