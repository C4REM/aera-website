What we collect

When you send an enquiry, through the form, by email or on Instagram, Aera keeps what you tell it: your name, your email, what kind of project it is, your answers about it and, if you book one, the time of your call.

If you become a client, Aera also keeps the details of the job, what was quoted and invoiced, and the messages about it.

Photos and video of people are personal data too. How those are handled is covered under Images below.

Why

To reply to you, quote, plan and deliver the work, and invoice for it. The legal basis is taking steps you asked for before a contract, then the contract itself, plus the legal duty to keep financial records.

Aera doesn’t sell your details, doesn’t add you to a mailing list, and doesn’t use them for anyone else’s marketing.

This website

There are no advertising or tracking cookies here, and no Google Analytics. The site remembers your showreel volume in your own browser, and nothing else.

The enquiry form briefly uses your IP address to stop spam (no more than five enquiries an hour); that record deletes itself after an hour.

Who else sees it

Only the Aera team working on your job, and the services that run the studio: Cloudflare, which hosts this site and stores enquiries; Apple iCloud Mail for email; and Meta if you message on Instagram. Each has its own data-protection obligations, and some process data outside the UK under approved safeguards.

How long

Enquiries that don’t turn into work are deleted within twelve months. Client records and invoices are kept for six years, because HMRC requires it.

Images

Aera keeps the copyright in what it shoots, and clients get full rights to use the delivered images for their own website, socials and marketing.

Aera may show work in its portfolio and on social media. If you’d rather it didn’t, or want it held back until a launch, just say, ideally before the shoot. Private events such as weddings are never posted without the host’s agreement.

If you appear in something Aera has posted and want it taken down, email and it will come down from Aera’s own site and channels.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what Aera holds about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it’s used. Email bookings@aerastudios.org and you’ll get an answer within a month.

If you’re not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
