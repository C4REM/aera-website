Green Planet Dubai

A day inside a biodome, photographing birds and animals that mostly did not want to be photographed.

Discipline

Year

Role

Notes
Competition entry

The shoot

Green Planet is an indoor rainforest in the middle of Dubai, and shooting in it is a genuinely strange problem: humid, dim under the canopy, and full of animals moving at their own pace regardless of yours.

It was for a photography competition, so the brief was mine to set. I went after personality and movement rather than clean field-guide portraits — light, composition, and a great deal of standing very still waiting.
